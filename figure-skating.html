Figure Skates, Blade Mounting & Sharpening in Maryland

Figure skaters at every level can walk into our Maryland shops for boots, blades and the work that makes them skate well together. We carry Jackson and Edea, mount blades, sharpen, and fit in person. No reservation is needed.

Boots and fitting

  • Jackson and Edea figure skates, fit in the shop with your foot in the boot.
  • Boot stretching and heat molding for comfort and a precise fit.

Blade mounting

  • Precise blade alignment matters. We mount figure blades in the shop with careful alignment.
  • Bring your blades and boots in and we will talk through the setup.

Sharpening and repairs

  • Hand sharpening for figure blades at all three shops.
  • Rivet and eyelet repairs to keep boots solid.

Frequently asked questions

Do you carry figure skates?

No. All services are walk-in at our shops in Laurel, Rockville and Odenton.

Yes, by hand, at every shop.

No. We fit figure skates in person at our shops.
