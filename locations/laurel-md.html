Hockey & Figure Skate Pro Shop in Laurel, Maryland

Our Laurel shop is our flagship pro shop for hockey players and figure skaters. Walk in with your skates and the team here will sharpen them by hand, fit new boots to your foot, heat mold them, and repair rivets and eyelets, all in the shop and all without a reservation.

What we do in the Laurel shop

  • Skate sharpening, done by hand while you wait
  • Skate fitting, heat molding and boot stretching
  • Rivet and eyelet repairs
  • Figure skates and blade mounting
  • Hockey sticks, protective gear and skates
  • Team apparel pickup for team stores

Brands we carry

Bauer, CCM, True, Warrior, Jackson and Edea.

Good to know

  • Walk-in only. Sharpening and fitting do not need a reservation.
  • Bring your skates, and tell us how you skate.
  • We fit skates in person and do not sell skates or gear online.

Who we serve

Laurel sits between Washington, DC and Baltimore, so skaters come to us from across the DMV. Skaters and players visit this shop from Laurel, Beltsville, Greenbelt, College Park, Bowie, Columbia, Fulton and the Washington, DC to Baltimore corridor.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a reservation for skate sharpening or fitting?

No. Sharpening and fitting are walk-in only. We do not take reservations.

How long does a sharpening take?

Usually about 15 minutes, longer during busy rushes before games and tournaments.

Do you work on figure skates?

Yes. We sharpen, fit and mount blades for figure skates, and carry Jackson and Edea.
