Returns & Refunds

Last updated October 17, 2026

We want you in gear that fits and works. If something is not right, here is exactly what we can do. Questions about a specific purchase? Call any of our three shops and we will help.

Returns on items bought in store

Days 1 to 11Refund to your original payment methodBring the item back with your receipt and we will refund the card or payment method you used.
Days 12 to 30Store creditAfter 11 days and up to 30 days from the purchase date, we can exchange the item or issue store credit for the amount you paid.
After 30 daysNo returnsWe cannot accept returns after 30 days. Manufacturer warranty claims are handled separately, so ask us.

To qualify, an item must be unworn, unused, clean and in its original condition and packaging, with the receipt or another proof of purchase.

What cannot be returned

  • Worn gear. Any gear that has been worn or used cannot be returned. This includes skates that have been skated on, heat molded, baked or sharpened, helmets and protective gear that have been worn, and clothing that has been worn or washed.
  • Personalized and team-store items. Items made for you, such as jerseys, jackets, hoodies, tees, hats and bags with a team logo, name or number, are custom and are final sale. We will fix any item that arrives defective or decorated incorrectly through our error (see below).
  • Services. Sharpening, heat molding, boot stretching, blade mounting and repairs cannot be refunded once performed. If you are unhappy with the work, tell us right away and we will make it right.

Defective items or our mistakes

If an item arrives damaged or defective, or we got the size, name, number or decoration wrong on a team order, contact us within 14 days of receiving it. We will repair, replace or refund it at no cost to you. Please keep the item and any packaging, and send a photo if you can.

How refunds are issued

  • Eligible refunds go back to the original payment method. After we receive and inspect the item, allow 5 to 10 business days for your bank or card company to post it.
  • Store credit is issued the same day we accept the return.
  • Shipping fees on online team orders are not refundable, unless the return is due to our mistake or a defect.
  • If you paid with cash, we refund with cash or store credit, your choice, within the first 11 days.

Team orders placed on this website

Team orders are confirmed with you by our staff before payment is taken, so please review sizes, names and numbers carefully. Once production starts on personalized items, changes and cancellations are no longer possible. You can cancel or change an order any time before production starts by calling the shop. Orders can be picked up at any of our shops or shipped for a flat fee, shown at checkout.

How to start a return: bring the item and your receipt to any East Coast Hockey & Skating Supply shop, or call us first. For online team orders, call the shop that handled your order or use the contact form on our website.

We may update this policy from time to time. The policy in effect on your purchase date applies to your purchase.
