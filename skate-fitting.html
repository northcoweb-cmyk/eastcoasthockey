Hockey Skate Fitting & Heat Molding in Maryland

Skate size does not match shoe size, and it changes between brands. The fastest way to a comfortable pair is a proper fitting. Walk into any of our three Maryland shops and we will fit you in person. We do not take reservations.

Proper skate fitting

  • We fit hockey and figure skates in the shop, with your foot in the boot, not from a chart.
  • We carry Bauer, CCM, True, Warrior, Jackson and Edea, so you can compare how different brands feel.
  • If you already know your model, we can still check the fit before you leave.

Heat molding (baking)

  • Heat molding shapes the boot around your foot for a glove-like fit and helps reduce hot spots and break-in time.
  • It works for hockey skates and figure skates alike.

Boot stretching and repairs

  • Boot stretching relieves pressure points so a good skate can feel right.
  • We repair rivets and eyelets to keep boots solid, often the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a reservation for a skate fitting?

No. Fittings are walk-in only at all three shops.

Should I buy skates online?

We do not sell skates or gear online. Skate sizing varies between Bauer, CCM, True, Warrior, Jackson and Edea, so we fit you in person.

What is heat molding?

Heat molding warms the skate so the boot shapes to your foot, which helps comfort and fit. We do it in the shop.

Which brands do you carry?

Bauer, CCM, True, Warrior, Jackson and Edea.
