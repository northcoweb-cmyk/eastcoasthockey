Skate Sharpening in Maryland: Walk In, No Reservation

A good edge changes how you skate. We sharpen hockey and figure skates by hand at all three of our Maryland shops, and you never need a reservation. Walk in, hand us your skates, and tell us how you skate.

How sharpening works here

  • Every pair is sharpened by hand and matched to the way you skate, not run through a one-size-fits-all setting.
  • Most sharpenings take about 15 minutes. During a game or tournament rush it can take longer, so give us a little extra time on busy weekends.
  • We sharpen hockey skates and figure skates, and we can check blades for damage while we have them.

When to get your skates sharpened

  • The edges feel dull or you slide on turns and stops.
  • You skated over something hard, like a floor, a puck or the rink door area.
  • You are switching to a new pair or new steel and want them set up right.
  • You are not sure. Ask us and we will look at the blades with you.

Tell us how you skate

  • Players and skaters have different preferences for bite and glide. Tell us your position, level and what feels off, and we will sharpen to match.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment for skate sharpening?

No. Sharpening is walk-in only at all three shops. We do not take reservations.

How long does a skate sharpening take?

Usually about 15 minutes. It can take longer during busy rushes before games and tournaments.

Do you sharpen figure skates?

Yes. We sharpen hockey skates and figure skates at every shop.

Where can I get my skates sharpened near me?

We have shops in Laurel, Rockville and Odenton (Piney Orchard), Maryland. Walk into whichever is closest.
