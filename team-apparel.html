Custom Hockey Team Apparel & Jerseys in Maryland

We are the team apparel supplier for hockey programs across Maryland. Each team has its own store on our website where players and families choose items, sizes, names and numbers. We decorate everything in house.

What teams order

  • Game jerseys and pant shells.
  • Warm-up jackets and pants.
  • Team hoodies, practice tees, hats and equipment bags.

How ordering works

  • Find your program in our team stores, choose the item and size, and add the player name and number where the item needs it.
  • Send the order request. We confirm the details with you before anything is made.
  • Pick it up at our Laurel, Rockville or Odenton shop, or choose shipping.

Personalized items

  • Items with a logo, name or number are made for you and are final sale, except when there is a defect or our mistake. See our returns policy for details.

Where to go

Frequently asked questions

How do team stores work?

Find your program in the team stores on our website, pick your gear, size, name and number, and send your order request. We confirm the details and arrange payment.

Can I pick up a team order in a shop?

Yes. Choose pickup at Laurel, Rockville or Odenton (Piney Orchard), or have it shipped.

My team is not listed. Can you set one up?

Yes. Send us your logo and colors from the contact form and we will help.

Can I return personalized team apparel?

Personalized items are final sale unless they arrive defective or we made a mistake. See our returns and refunds page.
